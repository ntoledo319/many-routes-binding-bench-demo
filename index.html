Try this

  1. Use A / D to move. Click “Move left” and press Q, then move with Q.
  2. Try giving the right direction the same key. The demo keeps the previous binding and shows the conflict.
  3. Press Escape to cancel capture. Reload the demo to reset its in-memory settings.

The free source is MIT licensed. It has two keyboard bindings and fixed controller movement. It does not include the full kit’s saved settings or controller-remapping code.
